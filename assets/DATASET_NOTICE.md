# About the images in `database/` and `test/`

The two folders contain photographs of identifiable people. Publish or
redistribute face images **only** with the consent of the people shown and with
permission to redistribute the pictures. Before this repository is made public,
the instructor must confirm that this applies to every image, or replace the
images.

Your own selfies (see the Readme) stay on your computer: do not commit or share
them without the same kind of consent.
